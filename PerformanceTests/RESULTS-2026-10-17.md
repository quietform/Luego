# Known-stall calibration — 2026-10-17

The custom `ReaderScroll` XCTest metric responds to deliberately blocked main-thread work. The exported frame counter remains zero, and the app-wide `XCTHitchMetric` does not respond in either tested process configuration. No quality gate or sustained-60-fps claim follows from this experiment.

## Control and conditions

The injector lives entirely in `PerformanceTests/Host/BenchmarkApp.swift`, outside the production app target. With `--benchmark-stall`, it schedules one synchronous 200 ms main-thread block 200 ms into each active scroll interval. It skips an interval that has already ended. A main-thread precondition, monotonic duration measurement, explicit status overlay, and `InjectedMainThreadStall` signposts provide evidence of the actual blocks. The normal mode injects nothing.

Both comparison runs use the same signed Release executable, normalized test configuration, offline text fixture, fresh process/in-memory database/top position before every pass, and three fast upward swipes through idle completion. Each records three samples after a discarded warm-up. Only the stall launch argument differs. All movement, completion, and stall assertions pass.

Device: iPhone 15, iOS 26.6.1 (23G83), 60 Hz; Xcode 26.0 (17A324), Swift 6.2, Release whole-module `-O`. Connection: paired local network. Focus was enabled by the user; neither accepted run logs a notification-banner interruption. Every pass reports nominal thermal state (`0`) before and after scrolling and Low Power Mode off. Those observations do not constitute continuous thermal sampling. No Instruments recording or build runs concurrently with the comparison.

## App-wide collector control

`testStableProcessStallCalibration` launches the app before constructing `XCTHitchMetric` and retains that process through warm-up and two recorded passes. It verifies three additional 200 ms blocks per pass. Its content position advances, so this is a collector diagnostic, not another matched baseline.

The app-wide count, ratio, and total duration remain zero in both samples. The custom signpost metric reports positive hitches: counts `1, 1`, ratios `45.7329, 6.5806` ms/s, and total-duration exports `116.4278, 16.6438` ms. Frame counts remain zero. Launching before metric construction and retaining the process does not resolve the app-wide result. Its internal cause remains unconfirmed; do not use it to certify zero hitches in this setup.

The test passes and the recording saves successfully; recording ends when the target exits after completion.

All three blocks are nested within matching `ReaderScroll` animation intervals, on the main thread of the benchmark process, with the expected subsystem/category. The trace contains 533 frame-lifetime rows and two hitch rows, both attributed to the benchmark process and labeled as potentially expensive app updates. Those two hitch rows begin shortly after the second and third blocks end. Frame-lifetime rows establish captured frame activity; they are not presented-frame counts.

This trace independently confirms injected main-thread work and late frames in that diagnostic run. Its two hitches are not replacements for any untraced XCTest value. Instrumentation and separate-run variability prevent equating the captures or inferring a production-method bottleneck.

## Verification and retained evidence

- TDD: the new control test first failed because the requested stall report was absent. After implementation, it passed with three actual blocks totaling at least 600 ms. The normal fixture test verifies zero blocks. Both checks pass on iPhone Air and iPad Pro 11-inch (M4) simulators, one booted at a time.
- Four Python checks pass. Both physical comparison tests, the stable-process diagnostic, and the traced functional test pass. Passing UI tests verifies execution and assertions, not the validity of every SDK counter.
- The first device attempt waited for unlock, completed warm-up, then timed out waiting for the first recorded scroll to complete. Its debug report remained `idle|0|0`; the cause is unconfirmed. It is retained as `initial-incomplete.xcresult` and excluded. The clean retry completed all required passes.
- The production app source and actor layout were not changed for calibration. The benchmark executable's Mach-O UUID is `417992D9-6964-3C88-8519-E4AF2B1917FE`; measurement and diagnostic products share that UUID. Later diagnostic changes affect the UI test runner.
